Getting a receipt from AlipayHK

Two ways. The first is easiest: tick the box while you give.

Option 1 — when you donate

Request a donation receipt and enter your personal details when donating.

  1. Step 1

    Tick 「需要收據」 (need a receipt) under the amount.

  2. Step 2

    The donor form appears — name in English, email, phone. Fill it in, then confirm the gift.

Option 2 — after you give

If you forgot to tick the box, you can still claim it from your donation records.

  1. Step 1

    Open AlipayHK and go to 慈善捐款.

  2. Step 2

    Tap 我的捐款 at the bottom, then 捐款記錄.

  3. Step 3

    Find the gift by the charity and the date.

  4. Step 4

    Tap 申請收據 and submit your details.

The app is shown in Chinese; the buttons sit in the same place on your screen.

Finding your AlipayHK transaction ID

When the payment completes, AlipayHK files it under Transaction History — the transaction ID is the long string of letters and numbers at the foot of that record.

  1. Step 1

    Open the payment again from Transaction History: the detail page repeats the merchant, the amount and the time.

  2. Step 2

    At the bottom of that page, the code above the barcode is the transaction ID. Copy the whole string and paste it here.
