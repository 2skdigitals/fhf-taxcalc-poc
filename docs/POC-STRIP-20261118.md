`qa/apply_pocstrip_20261118.py` (assert-once, re-runnable from
`embed/index.html.bak-pre-pocstrip-20261118`).

## What replaced the QR step

The two wallet rails are now **ordinary links** to
`https://www.hollows.org/zh-Hant-HK/ways-to-support/ways-to-give/` (the page that actually
carries the PayMe and AlipayHK instructions), and the card rail keeps its link to
`/zh-Hant-HK/donate/`. A comment in the markup marks where the merchant QR panel returns.

Every route on the block now works. Nothing on it is a placeholder, and nothing pretends
to accept money it cannot receive.
