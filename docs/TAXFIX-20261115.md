## 1 · The defect

```js
var savedLow  = Math.round(annual * rLow),
    savedHigh = Math.round(annual * rHigh);   // ← flat marginal multiplier
```

A flat `gift × marginal rate`. It is exact while the gift stays inside one tax
slice **and** under the 35% ceiling — which is why the small examples passed and
it survived review. Break either condition and it overstates:

- it never applies the **35% cap** on the deductible amount, and
- it never walks the **progressive slices** the deduction is taken from.

Measured against the six verified examples (skill §4), **two of six were wrong**:

| gift | assessable income | engine said | true saving | error |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HK$250,000 | HK$500,000 | **−HK$42,500** | **−HK$29,150** | **+45.8%** |
| HK$175,000 | HK$500,000 | **−HK$29,750** | **−HK$29,150** | +2.1% |
| HK$500 | HK$400,000 | −HK$85 | −HK$85 | exact |
| HK$1,800 | HK$600,000 | −HK$306 | −HK$306 | exact |
| HK$500 | HK$140,000 | −HK$0 | −HK$0 | exact |
| HK$10,000 | HK$2,500,000 | −HK$1,500 | −HK$1,500 | exact |

HK$42,500 is not a made-up number: it is the real saving for a HK$250,000 gift at
the **top** of the 17% band (income HK$2,132,500). The defect was presenting it
as the answer for the whole band, at every income in it, under a label that said
「未扣上限」 — *before* the cap — when the cap was never applied at all.

## 2 · The model now implemented (YA2026/27, single person)

Not re-derived from theory; written to the verified constants.

```
basic allowance          145,000
net chargeable income    max(0, income − 145,000)
progressive              50,000 slices at 2 / 6 / 10 / 14 %, then 17 % on the rest
standard                 two-tier on NET INCOME (not minus the allowance):
                         15 % on the first 5,000,000 + 16 % above it
tax payable              MIN(progressive, standard)
deductible               MIN(gift, 0.35 × income)
saving                   taxOn(income) − taxOn(income − deductible)
```

`taxOn(145,000) = 0`, `taxOn(2,132,500) = 319,875` both ways (the single-person
crossover), `taxOn(5,000,000) = 750,000` on the standard rate.

The maths lives in **one pure function**, `__fhfTax`, between the markers
`/* FHF_TAX_ENGINE_START */` … `/* FHF_TAX_ENGINE_END */` — no DOM, no globals,
so it can be extracted and run headlessly. It is exported as
`window.__fhfTax` (harmless inside an iframe, needed by the test).

`__fhfTax.saving()` returns the unrounded difference; the UI rounds for display.

## 3 · The two UI states

**Default — no exact income.** The income-range picker is unchanged. The saving
is now computed at **both ends of the selected band** (r0 = 145,000; r2 =
145,000–195,000; r6 = 195,000–245,000; r10 = 245,000–295,000; r14 =
295,000–345,000; r17 = 345,000–2,132,500; r15 = 2,132,500–5,000,000; r16 =
5,000,000–10,000,000) and rendered as a **range when the two differ**, a single
figure when they do not (only `r0` collapses, because both ends save nothing).
The caption states the source: 「範圍來自所選入息範圍的下限與上限。」

| band | HK$1,300 gift renders | why |
|---|---|---|
| r0 | −HK$0 | below the allowance, no tax |
| r2 | −HK$0 至 −HK$26 | band starts exactly at the allowance |
| r6 | −HK$26 至 −HK$78 | crosses 2 % → 6 % |
| r10 | −HK$78 至 −HK$130 | crosses 6 % → 10 % |
| r14 | −HK$130 至 −HK$182 | crosses 10 % → 14 % |
| r17 | −HK$182 至 −HK$221 | band starts on the 14/17 boundary |
| r15 | −HK$195 至 −HK$221 | see §7 — the crossover tie |
| r16 | −HK$195 至 −HK$208 | 15 % → 16 % |

「不確定」 (no band) keeps its analytic bounds: 0 % is reachable (any income at or
below the allowance pays no tax) and 17 % is the top marginal rate, so the range
is `HK$0 … gift × 17%`. It reads `0% – 17%` — the previous `2% – 17%` claimed a
floor the maths could not deliver.

**Optional exact income.** One collapsed row under the picker:

> ▸ 想更準確？輸入您的應評稅入息

A numeric field; filled, the result becomes a **single figure** computed exactly
(and the caption says so). Clearing it, or picking a band again, returns to band
mode — the two inputs never disagree about which one is in force. The value stays
in page memory: never posted, never pushed to `dataLayer`, never persisted.

## 4 · Copy that had to change

| before | why it was false | now |
|---|---|---|
| `預計節省稅款（未扣上限）` | it *was* capped — nothing was subtracted | plain `預計節省稅款` for a range / exact figure; `預計節省稅款（最多）` only where the cap has already limited a single figure |
| `…估算稅務回饋會低於 HK$29,750` | with the cap computed, the printed high end **is** the achievable figure | `…超出部分不能扣除；預計節省稅款 HK$29,150。` |
| `扣稅上限…實際節省的稅款會低於上列數字` | same | names the deductible amount: `可扣除 HK$175,000，超出部分不能扣除。` |
| `所節省的稅款等於捐款金額乘以您的適用稅率` (footnote) | only true under the cap and inside one slice | `在可扣除的金額之內，所節省的稅款約為該金額乘以您的適用稅率（最高 17%）。` |
| `估算採用的邊際稅率` on a band | the average of a band is not a marginal rate | `估算採用的稅率`; `邊際稅率` / `標準稅率` only when the whole deducted amount sat at one rate (within 0.06 pp) |

No reader can now say "that number is too high": every figure printed is a figure
the engine produced, and the label distinguishes a range from a computed maximum
from an exact number.

## 5 · Analytics — unchanged contract, one new coarse dimension

`calc_result_shown` gains `exact: "yes" | "no"`. Nothing else moved:
`band` is still the coarse id, `cap_state` is still `may_apply|unlikely` (now
derived from the engine rather than a HK$50,000 threshold), there is no amount,
no income and no rate anywhere in a payload. `qa/poc_test.py` types a distinctive
income (654,321) into the new field and re-scans every payload the frame ever
posted: neither `654321` nor `654,321` appears, and the only permitted trace is
`exact:"yes"`. Payment, receipt, GTM and the iframe guard are untouched.

## 7 · Two results a reader will question, and why they are right

1. **`r15` shows a higher saving at its floor than at its ceiling** (HK$1,300:
   −HK$195 至 −HK$221, i.e. 15 % → 17 %). At income exactly HK$2,132,500 the
   progressive and standard charges are equal (319,875); a deduction then tips
   the `MIN()` toward the progressive scale, so the marginal saving at that one
   income is 17 %. Above it the standard 15 % wins. The range is the honest
   consequence of evaluating both ends of the band, and the row is labelled
   `估算採用的稅率` rather than 標準 or 邊際 for exactly this reason.
2. **`r2` and 「不確定」 start at HK$0.** The floor of `r2` is the allowance
   itself, where there is no tax to save. A range that starts at zero is what the
   band actually contains; the old `2%` floor was an assumption.
