What counts as a deductible gift

Only money given to a charity approved under section 88 of the Inland Revenue Ordinance can be deducted. Money paid for something in return is not a donation — raffle tickets, auction purchases, dinner seats, sponsorship that buys advertising. At or below the basic allowance you pay no salaries tax, so a gift saves you nothing.

Can a couple combine the deduction?

Partly. What you cannot use — including the part above the 35% cap — your spouse may claim on their own BIR60. Their claim is capped at 35% of their own income or profits, so an unused deduction does not pass across in full.
