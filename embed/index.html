Finding your AlipayHK transaction ID

When the payment completes, AlipayHK files it under Transaction History — the transaction ID is the long string of letters and numbers at the foot of that record.

  1. Step 1

    The confirmation screen shows the amount and the charity — check it is the donation you just made, then tap Done.

  2. Step 2

    Open the payment again from Transaction History: the detail page repeats the merchant, the amount and the time.

  3. Step 3

    At the bottom of that page, the code above the barcode is the transaction ID. Copy the whole string and paste it here.
